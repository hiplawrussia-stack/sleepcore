## A3: Competitive Moat Details

### Critical Slowing Down (CSD)

Based on dynamical systems theory:
- Autocorrelation increase = system slowing
- Variance increase = instability
- Combined CSD index predicts transitions

Research: Smit et al. 2025 showed 32.9% sensitivity for depression recurrence, with EWS appearing **1+ month before** clinical transition.

### Thompson Sampling

Bayesian multi-armed bandit:
- Each message type = arm
- Beta distributions track success
- Exploration bonus prevents local optima

Research: DIAMANTE trial 2024 showed personalized messaging improves engagement by 40%+.
